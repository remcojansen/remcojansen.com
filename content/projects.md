A selection of things I've built or contribute to, mostly in and around the identity & access management space.

### OIDC-CLI

A command-line client for running OIDC flows, useful for testing and debugging identity provider configurations without needing a full application.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/jentz/oidc-cli)

### OpenID Client Migration

A migration tool built around a canonical client format, making it easier to move OpenID/OAuth client configurations between identity providers.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/remcojansen/openid-client-migration)

### Keycloak

Occasional contributions to the Keycloak project and the Terraform provider for Keycloak, helping improve tooling for teams that run Keycloak in production.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)
